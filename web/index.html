A CHECK, WITH A TRAIL
Four questions.
Every change in context.
01 / OUTCOMES
Did the primary outcome move?
Check endpoint definitions, timing and primary versus secondary designations.
02 / ANALYSES
Was the planned analysis reported?
Identify omissions and distinguish explicit explanations from assumptions.
03 / POPULATION
Who was actually studied?
Compare eligibility and analysis populations against the committed protocol.
04 / SAMPLE SIZE
Where did the numbers change?
Look for recruitment shortfalls, exclusions and their stated reasons.
Uncertainty stays visible.
Outcomes are ALIGNED, EXPLAINED_CHANGES, DEVIATIONS or INCONCLUSIVE. Missing, altered or unreadable evidence cannot produce a clean result. Every completed review attempt remains in a hash-linked history.
